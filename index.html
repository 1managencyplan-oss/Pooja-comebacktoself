What You'll Learn

6 Powerful Shifts That Can Change How You See Yourself

This isn't about fixing who you are. It's about uncovering what's been shaping you — and discovering a new way to move forward.

Shift 01
Decode Your Patterns
Discover why you keep repeating certain behaviours even when you consciously know better — and begin recognising the invisible patterns influencing your choices.
Shift 02
Uncover Hidden Beliefs
Explore the beliefs and meanings you may have carried for years — and notice how they could still be shaping your confidence, relationships, and decisions today.
Shift 03
Understand Your Triggers
Learn to see emotional triggers differently, recognise what may be happening beneath your reactions, and create more space between what you feel and what you do.
Shift 04
Interrupt the Autopilot
Discover a simple process to pause before automatically reacting, so you can begin responding with greater awareness, intention, and choice.
Shift 05
Rebuild Self-Trust
Explore how small, conscious choices can help you listen to your own voice, reduce constant second-guessing, and begin trusting yourself again.
Shift 06
Radiate From Within
Discover how authentic expression, conscious choices, and gratitude can help you reconnect with yourself and begin showing up more fully in your life.

Meet Your Mentor

Pooja Manocha

Inner Jewel Mentor · Comeback To Self

For years, Pooja worked in the world of jewellery, helping women choose beautiful jewels to wear. But her own journey taught her something deeper: a woman can look completely put together on the outside and still feel disconnected from herself within.

Through personal development and her exploration of inner-child work, EFT, somatic practices, mindset, and gratitude, Pooja began seeing her life through a new lens. Today, as an Inner Jewel Mentor, she helps women explore old beliefs and recurring patterns, practise new responses, and reconnect with greater self-trust and authentic expression.

Pooja once helped women wear beautiful jewels. Today, she helps them uncover the jewel within.
